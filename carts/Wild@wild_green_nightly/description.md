# Wild Green Nightly

**This is the test build of [Wild Green][cart].** It is the same cart, with
the changes that have not been cut into a release yet — so it is where a new
feature can be tried, and where a new feature can be broken.

Your stable cart is untouched. Every mod in this one installs under an id of
its own and **conflicts with its stable twin on purpose**: run one or the
other, never both. Nothing here can reach your Wild Green saves, your settings
or your cartridge.

The cartridge is **dark purple**, with `NIGHTLY` under the wordmark, so you can
tell at a glance which one you are opening.

## What is new in this one

### A dark mode, and a colourful one

`START > OPTION > UI THEME` — **`LIGHT`**, **`DARK`** or **`COLORFUL*`**.
`LIGHT` is the default and is exactly what the cart looked like before.

Every page the suite draws is black and white on purpose, so `DARK` is
literally that: paper black, ink white, and the two shades between them
swapped. It reaches the Pokédex, the box, the party menu, the bag, the mod
manager, the item screens and the suite's own settings — everything that draws
a full page. The overworld, the battles and the title screen are pictures
rather than pages and are left alone.

`COLORFUL` tints each screen by what it *is* — the Pokédex red, the box blue,
the party green, the bag leather — and colours the settings cards by what they
open, so you pick one by colour before you have read the word. The asterisk is
real: the battle command grid's four buttons are not coloured yet.

### The title screen is the right colour again

`WILD GREEN VERSION` read yellow-green on pale yellow, half the POKE BALL was
skin-coloured, and so was the highlight on the copyright line. All three were
the same bug in the same place, and all three are fixed in every display mode
— including `ADVANCED`, where none of the old fix reached.

### The version ribbon follows `PLAYER`

Put the character in purple and the title is lettered in purple. The words
still say `GREEN` — that is the game's name — but the ink is yours.

### `PLAYER` takes effect where you are standing

No relaunch. Change the colour and the walker changes under your feet, along
with the battle pics, the trainer card and the town-map marker.

## What is in it

The same four things the stable cart pins, two of them nightly:

- **Gen1WildQOL** — the released one, unchanged. Nothing in this nightly
  touches it, and a fork with no changes in it is a fork that only rots.
- **Crystal Animated Sprites with Shiny Visuals** — somebody else's mod,
  pinned as it is.
- **Gen1WildUI Nightly** — the visual half, plus `UI THEME`.
- **Wild Green Nightly** — the player in green, the names, and the title
  screen.

It is sealed like the stable cart, so it can still go online — with other
people running this same nightly.
